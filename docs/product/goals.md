# Goals & Non-Goals

## Goals

1. One common licensing platform for multiple product families.
2. Same codebase for SaaS and customer-hosted deployment.
3. Connected, intermittently connected and fully air-gapped operation.
4. Highly customized licensing without hard-coded feature columns.
5. Entitlement pooling with device-specific allocations and grants.
6. Hardware capability and combined-workload validation before activation.
7. Trial, purchase, add-on and renewal flows in one platform.
8. Traditional package OTA plus independent encrypted model OTA.
9. Automatic/manual update policies, maintenance windows and rollback.
10. Corporate-grade audit, approval and security controls.

## Phase 1 non-goals

- Full ERP/accounting capability.
- Windows-first implementation.
- Automatic customer-hosted LMS self-upgrade.
- Premature microservice decomposition.
- Claiming complete tamper resistance on a root-compromised edge host.
